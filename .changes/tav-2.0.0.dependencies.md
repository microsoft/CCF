Updated TAV to [2.0.0](https://github.com/microsoft/TEE-Attestation-Verification/releases/tag/tav-2.0.0). CBOR parsing through `<tav/cbor.hpp>`, including COSE envelopes and receipts handled by CCF, now rejects input nested deeper than 16 levels or containing maps with more than 128 entries, and failures are reported as `tav::Exception` with a `tav::ErrorCode`, replacing `tav::cbor::DecodeError`, `tav::cbor::EncodeError` and `tav::cbor::Error`.
