Every signature transaction of a service with an ML-DSA identity carries an ML-DSA COSE signature in `public:ccf.internal.cose_signatures` next to the classical one, and nodes replaying the ledger verify every COSE signature and require one for each identity in `public:ccf.gov.service.signing_identities`.
A node built without ML-DSA support aborts the recovery of such a ledger at its first ML-DSA-signed transaction or, if it replays none, at the end of the public ledger; see the [built-in maps reference](doc/audit/builtin_maps.rst) and the [7.x to 8.x migration guide](doc/releases/migration_7_x_to_8_x.rst#ml-dsa-service-identity).
